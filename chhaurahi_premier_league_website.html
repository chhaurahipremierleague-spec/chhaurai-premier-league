About CPL

Chhaurahi Premier League (CPL) is a village-level tennis-ball cricket tournament in Madhubani, Bihar, established in 2015. This page is intended to share official tournament announcements, team information, match results, original highlights and moments celebrating local players.

Official updates: Follow the CPL Facebook Page for confirmed dates, team entries, fixtures and results. Details will be posted here when announced by the organisers.
